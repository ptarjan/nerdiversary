Your first Saturn return is at age 29½

Saturn takes 29.46 Earth years to go around the Sun, so at about 29½ it is back where it was when you were born. That is your Saturn return. It is the same date as your first Saturn birthday: the day you turn 1 in Saturn years, one Saturn year being 29.46 Earth years. Astrologers tie it to the start of adulthood. Enter your birthday to get your date.

The math

  • Saturn’s orbital period is 10,759 Earth days, or 29.46 years
  • First return: age 29.5. Second: 58.9. Third: 88.4
  • Astrologers date the return from when Saturn gets back to its birth position in the zodiac. That can differ from the orbital date by a few months, because for about 4½ months each year Saturn appears to move backwards across the sky (retrograde) as Earth overtakes it

This site uses the orbital date. The same calculation gives your birthdays on every other planet, from Mercury (every 88 days) to Neptune (every 165 years).
