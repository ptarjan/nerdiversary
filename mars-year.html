Your age in Mars years is your Earth age ÷ 1.88

Mars takes 687 Earth days (1.88 Earth years) to go around the Sun once. That is one Mars year, so your age in Mars years is your Earth age divided by 1.88. Enter your birthday to get the date of each of your Mars birthdays.

The math

  • Mars orbits the Sun once every 686.98 Earth days
  • Age in Mars years = age in Earth days ÷ 686.98
  • A 30-year-old is 30 × 365.2425 = 10,957 days old, which is 15.9 Mars years

The same calculation works for every planet. A Mercury year is 88 Earth days, so Mercury birthdays come about four times a year. A Neptune year is 165 Earth years, so nobody has had one.
