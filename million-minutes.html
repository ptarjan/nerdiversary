A child turns 1 million minutes old at 1.9 years

A million minutes is 694 days, 10 hours and 40 minutes, or 1.9 years. A child reaches it about 5 weeks before their second birthday. Enter the birthday to get the date. For the exact minute, add the birth time in the full calculator.

The math

  • 1,000,000 minutes ÷ 1,440 minutes per day = 694.4 days
  • 694.4 days ÷ 365.2425 days per year (the average calendar year, counting leap days) = 1.90 years
  • For comparison, a million seconds is only 11.6 days

A 365-day year is 525,600 minutes, the number sung in “Seasons of Love” from the musical Rent. A million minutes is just under two of those.
