A child turns 1,000 days old at 2.74 years

1,000 days is 2.74 years, so a child reaches it in the week before turning 2 years and 9 months. Child-health researchers also talk about “the first 1,000 days”, but they count from conception, so their 1,000 days end around the second birthday. This page counts from birth.

The math

  • 1,000 days ÷ 365.2425 days per year (the average calendar year, counting leap days) = 2.74 years
  • That is about 8.9 months after the second birthday
  • The next round numbers: 2,000 days at 5.5 years, 5,000 days at 13.7 years

1,000 days is also 24,000 hours, or 86.4 million seconds.
