You turn 1 billion seconds old at 31.7 years

A billion seconds is about 31 years, 8 months and 8 days. No calendar marks it, so most people pass it without noticing. Enter your birthday to get your date. For the exact second, add your birth time in the full calculator.

The math

  • 1,000,000,000 seconds ÷ 86,400 seconds per day = 11,574 days, plus 1 hour, 46 minutes and 40 seconds
  • 11,574 days ÷ 365.2425 days per year (the average calendar year, counting leap days) = 31.69 years
  • Because of those extra 1 hour 46 minutes 40 seconds, birth time matters: born at 9:00 AM, you reach a billion seconds at 10:46:40 AM

A million seconds is 11.6 days. A billion seconds is 1,000 times longer: 31.7 years.
