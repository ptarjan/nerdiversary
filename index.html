Nerdiversary

Your age counted in seconds, planet years and powers of two

You turn 1 billion seconds old at about age 31.7. A year on Mars lasts 687 Earth days, so your first Mars birthday comes before your second Earth one. Enter a birthday to get the exact date and time of each milestone.

What gets counted

🪐

Planetary years

A year lasts 88 Earth days on Mercury, 687 Earth days on Mars and 84 Earth years on Uranus. You get the date of every birthday you reach on each planet.

🔢

Round numbers in base 10

You turn 1 billion seconds old at age 31.7, 10,000 days old at 27.4, and 1 million hours old at 114.

💻

Binary and hex

Round numbers in base 2 and base 16, the ones computers count in. You pass 2²⁰ (1,048,576) minutes just before your 2nd birthday, and 0x1000000 (16,777,216) seconds at about 6 months.

π

Mathematical constants

Constants such as π (3.14159) and e (2.71828), times a power of ten, counted in seconds. You pass e × 10⁸ seconds at age 8.6 and π × 10⁹ seconds at 99.6.

🌀

Fibonacci time

Each Fibonacci number is the sum of the two before it: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13. You turn 144, 233 and 377 days old, and each gap is longer than the last.

🔬

Scientific

Light from the moment you were born reaches the Moon 1.3 seconds later and Proxima Centauri, the nearest star, when you are about 4¼. Also counted: round numbers of new moons (one every 29.5 days), and multiples of 299,792,458 seconds, the speed of light in metres per second.

🎬

Pop culture

42 million seconds, after the answer in The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy, at about 16 months. Day 1,337, "leet" in gamer slang. Plus each Pi Day (March 14), Mole Day, May the 4th and other nerd holidays.

🔔

Notifications

Turn on notifications to get an alert a day before each milestone, an hour before, and at the minute it happens.

Dates are exact to the minute when you add a birth time, and shown in your device's time zone.
