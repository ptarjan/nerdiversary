Your half birthday is 182.6 days after your birthday

Your half birthday is the day you turn some-and-a-half years old (5½, 10½, 30½ and so on): 182.6 days after your birthday. Counting “same date, six months later” can be up to 2 days off, because months run from 28 to 31 days. Enter your birthday to get your half, quarter and three-quarter birthdays for every year.

The math

  • A year averages 365.2425 days once leap days are counted. Half of that is 182.62 days
  • Six calendar months range from 181 to 184 days, which is why the “same date” shortcut drifts
  • Quarter birthday: 91.3 days after your birthday. Three-quarter birthday: 273.9 days after

Children with summer birthdays often celebrate their half birthday at school instead, since their real one falls in the holidays.
