You turn 1,000 weeks old at 19.2 years

1,000 weeks is 7,000 days, or 19.17 years, so you turn 1,000 weeks old about 2 months after your 19th birthday. Enter your birthday to get the exact date.

The math

  • 1,000 weeks × 7 days = 7,000 days
  • 7,000 days ÷ 365.2425 days per year (the average calendar year, counting leap days) = 19.17 years
  • 2,000 weeks: 38.3 years. 3,000 weeks: 57.5 years. 4,000 weeks: 76.7 years

An 80-year life is about 4,174 weeks. Oliver Burkeman’s book on time management, Four Thousand Weeks, is named after that number.
