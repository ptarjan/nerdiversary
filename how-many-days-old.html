How many days old are you?

Enter your birthday and your age in days appears here, with weeks, hours, minutes and seconds counting up live. A round thousand days comes every 2.74 years, so you get one far more often than a birthday ending in zero.

The math

  • Days old = full days since midnight on your birth date, in your time zone
  • An 80-year life is about 29,220 days: 4,174 weeks, or 2.5 billion seconds
  • 10,000 days = 27.4 years. 20,000 days = 54.8 years. 30,000 days = 82.1 years

Some day counts are worth marking even though they are not round: day 11,111 (age 30.4), day 12,345 (age 33.8), and day 16,384 (age 44.9), which is 2¹⁴, a power of two.
